Abstract

Driven by the rapid evolution of Vision-Action (VA) and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, Imitation Learning has significantly advanced robotic manipulation capabilities. However, evaluation methodologies for these behaviors have lagged behind, hindering the standardization of the field. Current evaluation frameworks are impeded by two critical gaps: (1) Binary Evaluation Limitations, where metrics rely predominantly on task success rates, neglecting execution nuances such as smoothness and safety; and (2) Authenticity Verification Challenges, where distinguishing between genuine policy-generated actions and human teleoperation remains obscure, undermining the authority of real-world deployment claims. To bridge these gaps, we propose \(E_0\), a holistic evaluation solution comprising the Eval-Actions benchmark and the AutoEval architecture. First, anchored in "Human-Perceived Action Quality," we construct the Eval-Actions benchmark. Distinct from existing datasets restricted to human demonstrations, Eval-Actions innovatively integrates VA and VLA policy execution trajectories alongside human teleoperation data, explicitly including failure scenarios. This dataset is structured around three core supervision signals: Expert Grading (EG), Rank-Guided (RG) preferences, and Chain-of-Thought (CoT). Building on this, we propose the AutoEval architecture: AutoEval-S leverages Spatio-Temporal Aggregation for semantic assessment, augmented by an auxiliary Kinematic Calibration Signal (\(I_{phys}\)) to refine motion smoothness, achieving state-of-the-art performance in scoring and ranking tasks; AutoEval-Plus incorporates the Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) paradigm to enhance logical reasoning capabilities. Experimental results demonstrate that AutoEval exhibits exceptional evaluation precision, achieving Spearman's Rank Correlation Coefficients (SRCC) of 0.81 and 0.84 under the EG and RG protocols, respectively, with corresponding \(R_{\ell_2}\) errors of 3.45 and 3.49 on the Eval-Actions benchmark. Crucially, the framework possesses robust source discrimination capabilities, distinguishing between policy-generated and teleoperated videos with 99.6% accuracy. This capability provides authoritative Authenticity Verification for real-world robotic performance, effectively establishing the credibility of evaluation results.
